I am Marc Riera, an infrastructure engineer and architect based near Cambridge, UK.

I started in 2007 running Linux, mail, backup and a compute grid for a
research centre in Barcelona. From 2011 I installed HPC clusters for a living: Slurm,
InfiniBand, Lustre, GPUs, acceptance tests with a spreadsheet and awk. From 2016 I ran
archive, transfer and identity services for a research institute, as the archive grew
past 150 petabytes. Along the way I picked up a team, a budget and a longer job title.

I still build things. Today that means a three-host ZFS fleet, one GPU shared between too
many jobs, an InfiniBand fabric that exists only in containers, and a handful of apps I
have shipped. I am looking for technical work in HPC and AI infrastructure.
