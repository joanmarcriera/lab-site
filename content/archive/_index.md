This is my first blog, which ran at `blog.joanmarcriera.es` from 2009 to 2014 under the
title *3000 || 215\**. I wrote it for myself and for the researchers I supported: short
notes on whatever I had just fixed, in whichever of three languages I was thinking in that day.

The site went offline years ago. I recovered the posts from a WordPress export and from the
Internet Archive, and the old addresses now redirect here. Each post is as I wrote it. I
removed names, addresses and anything else that pointed at a real system, repaired the
formatting, and added a short **2026 note** on what has changed since. Most screenshots
did not survive. A few posts were copies of other people's articles, as was common then;
those now carry a short description and a link to the original instead.

Some of it has aged badly, which is rather the point of keeping it.
