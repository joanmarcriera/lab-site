## 2026 note

gnome-blog was a small GNOME panel applet for posting to blogs over XML-RPC, and it is no longer maintained or packaged by current distributions. WordPress today has its built-in editor and a REST API, and desktop or mobile clients talk to it through that or through XML-RPC, which needs to be enabled on the site.
