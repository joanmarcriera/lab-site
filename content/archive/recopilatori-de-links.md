## 2026 note

These links are from 2010 and several are certainly dead or have moved; for example Scroogle was shut down in 2012 and the Sun Grid Engine wiki disappeared after Oracle took over Sun. For HPC job scheduling, Slurm is today the most widely used scheduler, replacing the Torque and Grid Engine setups listed here.
