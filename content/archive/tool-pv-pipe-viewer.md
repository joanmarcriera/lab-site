This post was a copy of the catonmat.net article "A Unix Utility You Should Know About: Pipe Viewer" by Peteris Krumins. It showed how `pv` puts a progress bar, throughput and ETA into pipelines such as gzip, tar and nc. The original is at [catonmat.net](https://catonmat.net/blog/unix-utilities-pipe-viewer).

## 2026 note

`pv` is still maintained and packaged in all major distributions and in Homebrew.
