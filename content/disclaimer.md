These are personal reflections on a career in infrastructure and technology
leadership. Please read them in that spirit, and with the following in mind.

**They are not about any specific employer, and they do not name one.** Where a
post refers to "the institute", "the organisation" or a former workplace, that
is a deliberately generic stand-in. Nothing here should be read as a statement
about, or on behalf of, any named company, institution, or funder.

**They are stories, and stories are shaped.** To make a point land, I compress
timelines, merge similar events, change and remove details, and invent
connective tissue. Characters such as "a manager", "an engineer" or "a new
joiner" are composites and illustrations, not portraits. **Any resemblance to a
real, identifiable person is unintended and should not be assumed** — if you
think you recognise someone, you are almost certainly reading a real person into
an invented one.

**Do not take the specifics at face value.** Numbers, quotes, dates and
technical particulars are chosen to serve the lesson, not to document history.
The value is in the general principle, not in any claim about what exactly
happened, when, or to whom.

**Everything described is in the past, and none of it is current.** Where a
post touches on weaknesses — in security, process, or organisation — those were
addressed at the time, and the environments concerned have moved on since under
people doing good work. I have no current access to any former employer's
systems and no knowledge of their present state, and nothing here should be
read as a description of one. Several posts were revised in September 2026 to
remove detail that could have pointed at particular people or places; the
lessons are unchanged.

**On authorship.** The experiences, opinions, conclusions and source material
are mine. I use AI for editing, structure, source discovery and drafting
assistance, and I review and approve every published claim.

**These are my views alone**, written in a personal capacity, and do not
represent any current or former employer. The opinions, and the responsibility for them, are mine.

If anything here reads as pointing at a particular person or organisation, that
was never the intent — and I would rather hear from you than have you wonder. You
can reach me through the [lab repo](https://github.com/joanmarcriera/lab-site).
